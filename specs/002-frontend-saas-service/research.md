# Research: Frontend SaaS Service

## Research Tasks

- **Frontend testing framework**: Research and decide on a suitable frontend testing framework for a React/TypeScript project.
- **Performance Goals**: Define specific performance goals for the service, such as response time, throughput, and error rate.
- **Constraints**: Identify any technical or business constraints that may impact the implementation.
- **Scale/Scope**: Define the expected scale and scope of the service, such as the number of users, data volume, and feature set.
